Performance & Architecture

Engineered as a Native Desktop Tool

Fast, local, and resource-efficient. Starora executes neural network inference directly on your graphics card.

Vulkan Compute Engine

Utilizes Vulkan NCNN shaders for maximum hardware throughput on NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel GPUs. Includes automatic fallback to CPU processing.

Video Super-Resolution

Extracts frames with multi-threaded FFmpeg, batches restoration tasks across GPU compute queues, and preserves original audio tracks without fidelity degradation.

Interactive Timeline & Zoom

Dedicated canvas with fluid mouse wheel zoom up to 1000%, drag-to-pan inspection, and an interactive video timeline scrubber to preview exact frames.

GFPGAN Face Enhancement

Integrated face restoration model detects human faces and synthesizes sharp facial features, restoring blurred eyes and expressions in family portraits.

Smart Storage Compression

Configurable rate control with CRF 18 / 23 / 28 video encoding and level 9 PNG or WebP compression to save storage space without degrading perceived fidelity.

100% Offline & Private

Your media never leaves your workstation. No external telemetry, no remote cloud processing, and completely operable in isolated network environments.
