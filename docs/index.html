Core Capabilities

Designed as a focused desktop workstation rather than a cloud API client.

Local Vulkan Acceleration

Runs directly on your discrete GPU or integrated graphics via Vulkan compute shaders. No cloud queue, no bandwidth costs, and zero data leaves your computer.

Video Super-Resolution

Integrated with FFmpeg to extract frames, process each frame in parallel batches, and re-encode to H.264, H.265 (HEVC), or ProRes while preserving the original audio.

Interactive Scrubber & Zoom

Dedicated canvas with smooth mouse wheel zoom up to 1000%, drag-to-pan navigation, and an interactive video timeline scrubber to preview exact frames.

GFPGAN Face Restoration

Restores blurred facial features, expressions, and fine skin details in old photographs or low-resolution portrait scans.

Smart Rate Compression

Flexible output control with CRF 18 / 23 / 28 video encoding and level 9 PNG or WebP compression to save storage space without degrading perceived fidelity.

Self-Contained Portable

Binaries for Real-ESRGAN NCNN and FFmpeg are embedded into the application. Ready to run right after extraction with no extra dependency installation.
