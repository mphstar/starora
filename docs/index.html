Capabilities

Built for Performance & Privacy

Everything runs directly on your machine. No cloud subscriptions, no data leaks, and no artificial file size caps.

Image Super-Resolution

Upscale photos, art, and illustrations up to 4x native resolution with realistic texture synthesis and sharp edge recovery.

Video AI Processing

Multi-threaded video frame upscaling with built-in FFmpeg engine. Includes interactive timeline scrubber and zero audio loss.

GFPGAN Face Restoration

Automatically detects and repairs low-resolution faces in old photographs, removing blur and restoring natural expressions.

Vulkan GPU Compute

Harnesses modern Vulkan NCNN shaders for fast parallel execution across NVIDIA GeForce, AMD Radeon, and Intel Arc GPUs.

100% Offline & Private

Your creative work never leaves your storage drive. Works seamlessly without an active internet connection.

Smart Storage Compression

Configurable rate control with CRF 18 / 23 / 28 and WebP/PNG level 9 compression to shrink file sizes by up to 80%.

AI Models

Specialized Neural Networks

Pre-trained deep learning architectures tailored for different types of visual media.

Real-World Media

realesrgan-x4plus

The standard model for photographs, portraits, landscape captures, and textured real-world imagery. Synthesizes rich high-frequency textures.

Illustration & 2D

realesrgan-x4plus-anime

Trained specifically on manga, anime, and stylized line art. Prevents halo artifacts and renders razor-sharp contours.

Noise Reduction

realesrnet-x4plus

Features an integrated denoising pipeline. Excels at restoring degraded digital captures with high ISO noise or compression grain.
